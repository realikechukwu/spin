# Delivery notes

**If nobody engages the first few times, keep going anyway.** It usually takes about three weeks of consistent delivery before people start bringing their own material. The silence at the start is not a verdict on the idea.

**Invite one contribution at the end.** "Anyone had something similar?" converts a monologue into a habit much faster than repetition alone.

**Keep a running log.** One line per session: date, topic number, and anything anyone raised in response. Directly usable as teaching evidence for your ARCP portfolio, and it stops you repeating yourself.

**Get the timing right.** These work best at the end of handover once the clinical list is closed, not in the middle of it. Ninety seconds is the ceiling. If you overrun, people stop staying.
