# The 90-Second Handover Teaching Rota

**41 teaching points across seven systems.** One concept each, delivered in under two minutes at the end of handover.

Live version: `index.html` in this repo. Numbering here matches the numbering in the app, so No. 14 is the same topic in both.

---

## How to use this

Spin the wheel, or pick a number from the table below. Every topic is written to the same five beats:

| Beat | What you say | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | "Quick one from last night" or "this catches people out" | 5 sec |
| Principle | The physiology or logic in one sentence | 15 sec |
| Rule | Two or three concrete numbers or actions | 30 sec |
| Trap | The specific error it prevents | 15 sec |
| Source | So people can check it themselves | 5 sec |

**Three rules that make it land:**

1. **Anchor it to a real patient from that shift if you can.** "The 78-year-old in bay 4 last night" beats an abstract principle every time.
2. **One number only.** Give five numbers and people retain none. Give one and they carry it.
3. **Lead with the principle, not the guideline.** People forget "NICE says X". They remember why X is true.

**Check anything guideline specific against your local trust protocol before you say it out loud.** Pathways diverge most on troponin timing, paracetamol regimens, hyperkalaemia protocols and sepsis bundles.
